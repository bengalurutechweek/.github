# Bengaluru Tech Week

Bengaluru Tech Week is a citywide builder week for founders, engineers, researchers, designers, operators, investors, students, and community organisers.

The inaugural edition ran from **September 1 to 6, 2026**, bringing independently hosted meetups, workshops, demos, office hours, roundtables, showcases, and community gatherings into one shared citywide calendar.

The next Bengaluru Tech Week runs from **August 2 to 8, 2027**, with Aarambha on August 1 as Day 0.

## How The Week Works

Bengaluru Tech Week is a shared citywide layer rather than one central conference. Hosts keep their own identity, format, registration, and audience. The common website and calendar help builders discover the people, ideas, and communities gathering across the city.
